Original reference

Xorex

Arrow-key fluency, not a race against animation.

Left / right
Select a lane. No wrapping.
Down
Gather the bottom same-color run, or convert held gems into charge. Black cannot be gathered.
Up
Return held gems to the lane, or spend one charge to shoot one bottom gem, any color.

Matching colors add charge. A mismatched white run adds 5 × its size². Other mismatches destroy both stores.

New gems arrive at the top. A lane longer than 14 ends play on the next simulation frame.

Or press Up / Down. The starting key does not also act.
Ready

Tap keys: holding a key does not repeat. Actions commit immediately; arrivals run at 30 Hz. Hidden tabs suspend simulation without catching up.

Developer extras and fidelity notes

These controls are not original game mechanics. Pause and frame-step are inspection aids; the game also suspends while this tab is hidden.
