Xorex FX

Collect. Convert. Clear a path. A preservation-first revival of Adam M. Smith’s 2009 jewel-matching shooter, built around fluent arrow-key play.

The current playable experiment

Precise original-derived rules, luminous spheres, action feedback that leaves a memory, and an experimental explainable autoplayer. Play yourself, or press Tab to toggle the bot. Any manual arrow takes control.

Play Xorex FX

Keyboard recommended. Left/right selects a lane; Down collects or converts; Up replaces or fires. Silent by default. The bot is experimental, not a perfect player.
